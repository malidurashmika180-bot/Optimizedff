MALIYA – Free Fire Sensitivity Optimizer

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the maliya_freefire_optimizer folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.

FILES
- index.html: website structure
- style.css: design and responsive layout
- script.js: device info check and sensitivity profile generator

NOTES
- The scanner reads only information exposed by the browser. It cannot reliably detect every phone model, actual RAM, GPU, refresh rate, or benchmark performance.
- Sensitivity values are illustrative starting recommendations, not guaranteed headshot settings or official game values.
- The Google Fonts connection needs internet; the site otherwise works locally.
